# Photo to sketch practice

Turn personal photo references into sketch studies the way observational comic/illustration artists work.

## Ladder photoshoot (engage the subject)
1. Large view -- subject in environment
2. Move closer -- mid / portrait
3. Micro -- the "gosh, look at that" details

Prefer the user's own photos over endless stock hunting.

## Sketch passes
1. **Layout** -- size, proportion, dominant simple shapes (keep ridiculously simple)
2. **Structure** -- main contours / materials / supporting shapes
3. **Clarify** -- for coloring pages: open ink outlines; for studies: contrast, optional light screentone/hatch -- not muddy photo-trace

## Rules
Interpret and communicate -- don't mindlessly trace everything. Build outside-in, shape -> texture. Multi-angle perception beats one thumbnail.
