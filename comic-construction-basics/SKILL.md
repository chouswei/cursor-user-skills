# Comic construction basics

Core craft that separates stiff amateur drawings from usable comic/illustration construction -- for likeness gifts and sequential art.

## Three pillars
1. **Storytelling intent** -- clear focal point, composition serves the read (Rule of Thirds, leading lines, negative space). On a single coloring plate, the face/subject is the focal point.
2. **Anatomy / structure in action** -- gesture and simple 3D forms (sphere/box/cylinder) before contours; weight and proportion; foreshortening when angled.
3. **Consistency** -- same character readable from many angles; lighting/scale coherent if environments appear.

## Step habits
- Forms / stick / cylinders when stuck; gesture when confident
- Localized studies for weak parts
- Clean ink pass over rough underdrawing
- Hair: hairline -> mass -> flow -> detail
- Cross-hatch/screentone from shadow toward light, respecting form -- sparse on kids coloring finals unless asked
