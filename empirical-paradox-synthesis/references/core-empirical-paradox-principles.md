# Core principles: empirical paradox synthesis

## Intent

- **Empirical paradox synthesis** = name the **tension** (two valid pulls), anchor **each pole in evidence or hard constraint**, then produce a **both/and** resolution that is **checkable** against data - not a vague compromise.
- Differs from **scientific-method-first-principles**: that skill **runs the full scientific method** and fuses evidence with bedrock invariants into a single design. This skill **starts from opposing truths** and synthesizes under empirical discipline.

## Rules

- **No pole without support:** Each side of the tension must cite **observed behavior, measurement, SLO, incident, or explicit non-negotiable** (tag E or O). If support is missing, label "hypothesis" and name the measurement that would ground it.
- **Paradox is real:** Do not flatten the tension prematurely; synthesis must **acknowledge** why both sides matter.
- **Synthesis types (pick what fits):** phased trade (time-split), scoped contexts (different surfaces), guardrailed automation, tiered SLOs, architecture seam (interface that satisfies both under different assumptions), or explicit **accepted debt** with kill criteria.
- **Falsifiable:** The resolution should imply **predictions** (what metrics improve, what regressions to watch).
- **No fabricated data:** Placeholders + required experiments only.

## Anti-patterns

- "Balance" without a mechanism.
- Ignoring contradictory measurements.
- Synthesis that violates stated invariants without calling it out.

## Related skills

- Pure dialectic without empirical anchoring: use **scientific-method-first-principles** (frame hypotheses) or **mcdm-decider** (rank options); do not invent a soft compromise.
- Evidence + invariant fusion without mandatory paradox pass: **scientific-method-first-principles**.
